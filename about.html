The Schottenstein Homes Team

Est. 2007

Founded in 2007 by industry veterans Paul Coppel and Steven Schottenstein, Schottenstein Homes builds on nearly 50 years of combined experience and a legacy of constructing and selling more than 70,000 homes in communities across the nation.

At the heart of everything we do is a commitment to customer satisfaction. From selecting only the finest land for development to carefully crafting homes that reflect the unique needs and desires of each buyer, we take pride in every step of the process.

If you’re searching for a new home in Central Ohio, we invite you to explore our homes online, reach out to our team, or visit one of our communities for a personal tour—you’ll quickly see why Schottenstein Homes is one of the most trusted names in homebuilding.
