Engineering & Materials

Built to Endure,
Detailed to Delight

Every home begins with materials and systems chosen for performance as much as presence. A high-efficiency Bryant furnace and air conditioning system hold the interior at an even, quiet comfort, while individual air ducts carry that comfort consistently from room to room. Vinyl windows fitted with low-E glass are set with the precision of fine joinery, and every wall is finished with insulation applied with the same attention to detail, ensuring lasting beauty and durability.

Beneath the surface, Tyvek housewrap and flashing guard against air and water intrusion, and a durable waterproofing membrane shields the foundation from the ground up—engineering, largely unseen, that is simply built to last.

Paul Coppel
