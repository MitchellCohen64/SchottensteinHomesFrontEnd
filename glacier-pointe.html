Glacier Pointe

Plain City, OH · Jonathan Alder Local Schools

About Glacier Pointe

Glacier Pointe is a Schottenstein Homes community in Plain City, OH, zoned for Jonathan Alder Local Schools. Floor plans here start at $449,900, with designs ranging from 1,760–2,621 square feet.

Which schools serve Glacier Pointe?

Homes in Glacier Pointe are currently zoned for Jonathan Alder Local Schools. Buyers should confirm current attendance boundaries and school assignments directly with the district.

What is it like to live in Plain City?

Plain City offers a small-town setting with convenient access to parks, shopping, dining, and employment centers throughout the northwest Columbus area.Visit the Village of Plain City website
