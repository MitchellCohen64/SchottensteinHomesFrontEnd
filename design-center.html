Design Center

What is the Schottenstein Homes Design Center?

The Schottenstein Homes Design Center conveniently showcases numerous design options under one roof. You’ll have your very own Personal Design Consultant, who is available to provide guidance and advice to help you achieve the desired design style of your new home in Columbus, Ohio.

You decide which features are most important to you, and we’ll help you create your very own personalized home. Our design options are so expansive that no two homes in any of our communities end up looking the same—take a virtual tour and see for yourself.

Contact us today or stop by our design center to see everything that Schottenstein Homes has to offer.

What You’ll Find

Designing your home should be simple and enjoyable. At our Design Center, you’ll find quality selections from trusted manufacturers, all built to the highest standards of style, durability, and energy efficiency. Meet with a Personal Design Consultant or tour our homes for inspiration to bring your vision to life.

Exteriors

Choose from quality vinyl siding in rich wood-grain colors, custom front doors, and stone or brick accents for standout curb appeal. Finish the look with shutters and coach lights.

Cabinets & Countertops

Quality Aristokraft cabinets in a wide range of styles and finishes—from timeless whites to rich stains—paired with laminate, granite, or quartz countertops.

Flooring & Wall Finishes

A wide selection of flooring—wood, carpet, vinyl, tile, and more—to match your lifestyle, complete with walls and trim in your choice of colors.

Sinks, Tubs, and Accessories

Choose from stainless or farmhouse sinks, faucets in a range of finishes, and coordinating touches like towel bars and shower doors for a cohesive design.

Lighting

From outdoor fixtures to sparkling chandeliers, a wide range of energy-efficient lighting options—curated or fully custom—to finish your home.

Fireplaces & Stair Railing

Create a stunning focal point with fireplaces customized by mantles and surround tiles, then elevate your space with stair railings in a variety of finishes.

F.A.Q.

We know buying a new home comes with questions, and we’re here to make the process clear and stress-free. Browse our FAQs to find quick answers about our building process, design options, warranties, and more.

What happens when I visit the Schottenstein Homes Design Center?

Your Design Consultant will be there to help you select finishes based on your personal style, define your vision of your home, and work within your desired budget. Your time in our design center is made to be interactive, exciting, and fun.

How do I prepare for my Schottenstein Homes Design Center appointment?

Think about how you’ll live in each room, and collect photos and anything else that will help you and your design consultant define your style. If you have children, arrange for a babysitter—we need homebuyers to give their undivided attention to their appointment.

How do my choices affect the cost of my home?

That’s the true value of designing your own home. You pay only for the things that you want to include in the construction of your new home in Columbus. There are also many no-cost choices, and all prices include tax, delivery, installation, and warranty.

How much should I expect to spend on my design choices?

We realize that no two families are the same, and every homebuyer’s desired budget is different. You decide how much to spend on your home’s unique design, and your Design Consultant will work within that desired budget.

Are my design choices added to the purchase price?

Except for custom electrical and custom lighting choices, everything is added to the purchase total, providing you the maximum flexibility in structuring your finances for your new home in Columbus, Ohio.

Can I go to the Schottenstein Homes Design Center before I even purchase a home?

Absolutely! Browsing the Schottenstein Homes Design Center is a terrific way to get a feel for what it means to create a home. Feel free to stop by during browsing hours or schedule a formal preview through your sales representative!
